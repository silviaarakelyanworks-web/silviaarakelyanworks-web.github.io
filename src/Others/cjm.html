Experience
The notification arrived quickly
The wait was short — the push already contained all the needed info
Opening the site you immediately see the notification and know where to find the history
I go to favourites to check the address and build a route
Venue photos are easy to find — no need to scroll and search
They address me by name and confirm the number of guests
Good food and atmosphere — just like the photos on Afisha-Restaurants
After booking, Afisha-Restaurants has no further contact with the user — no discounts, promotions or bonuses
Pain points
Reservation isn't confirmed immediately — you have to wait with no indication of how long
Taxi call was removed — you have to copy the address manually
Can't build a route inside Afisha-Restaurants — have to switch between the map app and the site, ending up navigating by map only
Reviews can only be written if you liked the venue. Review link is buried at the bottom of the page
